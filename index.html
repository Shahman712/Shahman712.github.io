About MBanu

MBanu is a singer born on July 12, 1992, in Korla, Xinjiang, China. She performs in Uyghur and Uzbek, sharing her music with audiences across different linguistic and cultural backgrounds.

Her musical work focuses on pop music and original songs. Since April 2026, her releases have been published in Uzbekistan through Muzalar. This website presents her biography, music, and artistic journey.

Biography

MBanu is a singer and songwriter born on July 12, 1992, in Korla, Xinjiang, China. She performs in both Uyghur and Uzbek, expressing her artistic identity through music and emotional storytelling. Her musical style focuses on pop music and original songs. Since April 2026, MBanu has been releasing her original music in Uzbekistan through Muzalar. MBanu writes the lyrics and music for her songs, reflecting her personal creativity and musical vision. The post-production of her releases is handled by MuhammadAli Hamidov, with whom she shares a strong professional collaboration. She considers him an important creative partner and looks forward to continuing their work together. Through her music, MBanu aims to connect with listeners across cultures and languages while developing her own artistic style.
