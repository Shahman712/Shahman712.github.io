About MBanu

MBanu is a singer and songwriter born on July 12, 1992, in Korla, Xinjiang, China. She performs in Uyghur and Uzbek, sharing her music with audiences across different linguistic and cultural backgrounds.

Her musical work focuses on pop music and original songs. According to her artist biography, her releases have been published in Uzbekistan through Muzalar since April 2026.

Biography

MBanu expresses her artistic identity through music and emotional storytelling. As a singer and songwriter, she writes lyrics and music for her original songs.

The post-production of her releases is handled by MuhammadAli Hamidov, with whom she has a professional creative collaboration.
