About MBanu

MBanu is a singer born on July 12, 1992, in Korla, Xinjiang, China. She performs in Uyghur and Uzbek, sharing her music with audiences across different linguistic and cultural backgrounds.

Her musical work focuses on pop music and original songs. Since April 2026, her releases have been published in Uzbekistan through Muzalar. This website presents her biography, music, and artistic journey.
